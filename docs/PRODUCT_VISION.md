# Product vision

The repository describes Yugm as an Indian live social discovery app centered on finding people and shared experiences, with “Match the vibe.” as its tagline. Source and README material show capabilities for discovery, profiles, messaging, social relationships, live audio/video rooms, events, gifts, creator payouts, and safety.

This is a summary of existing product language, not a newly approved roadmap or audience expansion. Confirm new product requirements with the owner. Existing trust verification and payout KYC are distinct systems; see [project context](PROJECT_CONTEXT.md) and the linked policy documents in the [index](INDEX.md).
